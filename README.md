# The Tuesday World — 24 November 2026

One image that sums up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026:
the world itself is the design. A dot-matrix world map drawn from real
Natural Earth land data, with a numbered pin at the real location of
every story — numbered to match the stories below it.

- Lead: Nvidia brings back Jensen Huang as chief executive after Trump
  steps in (Santa Clara, 23 Nov).
- Then: the Gaza strike that kills Hamas's security chief (24 Nov),
  NATO's vow to defend space (Berlin, 23 Nov), Russia's "open war"
  warning to Estonia (Tallinn, 23 Nov), Ukraine defending the sinking
  of a Russian warship off Benghazi (24 Nov), the magnitude 5.6
  earthquake in Nepal (Taplejung, 24 Nov), and the Solomon Islands
  dropping its China security pact (Honiara, 24 Nov).

The map is drawn from Natural Earth 110m land data (public domain),
and every pin sits at the real latitude and longitude of its story.

Every fact was checked in-session against a named source (Reuters,
Xinhua), and each story carries its own date and source in the image.

`main.ts` copies the rendered `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to
`/output/entry.png` after verifying it is a real PNG inside the limits.
It runs with no input, no network, and writes the same bytes every time.
